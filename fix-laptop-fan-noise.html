Fix Loud Laptop Fan Noise

A loud laptop fan usually means your system is overheating or under heavy load.

Common Causes

  • Dust inside cooling vents
  • High CPU usage
  • Old thermal paste

Fix 1: Clean the Fan

Dust buildup restricts airflow and forces fans to spin faster.

Fix 2: Reduce CPU Load

Close unnecessary programs and background processes.

Fix 3: Replace Thermal Paste

This improves heat transfer from CPU to heatsink.
