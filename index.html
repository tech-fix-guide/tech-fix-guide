How to Fix Slow Windows 11

Updated: February 21, 2026

1. Disable Startup Apps

Too many startup apps can slow boot time and performance.

2. Free Up Disk Space

Delete temporary files and unused programs.

3. Update Drivers

Outdated drivers often cause lag and freezes.

Final Thoughts

Following these steps should noticeably improve system speed.
